# Changelog

## 1.0

- Real-time mono and polyphonic audio-to-MIDI in Audio Unit v2, VST3 and standalone for Apple Silicon.
- First-cycle mono detection, timed acquisition, pick retriggering and mono pitch bend.
- Host MIDI output and per-instance virtual CoreMIDI routing.
- English and German interface, preset bank and configurable defaults.
- A4 reference presets for 432 Hz and 440 Hz alongside the adjustable tuning knob.
- Two performance photos, a dark Moonshroom Studio logo panel and four website links.
- Current 1.0 packages and checksums under the v1.0 release.
